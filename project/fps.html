FPS Guide

Improving your FPS can make your games smoother and give you a better gaming experience.

Lower Graphics Settings

Lowering some graphics settings can help your computer produce more frames per second.

Close Background Programs

Closing programs that you are not using can free system resources for your game.

Keep Your Drivers Updated

Updated graphics drivers can help your computer work correctly with modern games.

Choose the Right Resolution

A lower resolution can sometimes improve performance when your computer is struggling.
