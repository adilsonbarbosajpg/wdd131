Sensitivity Guide

Your mouse sensitivity can have a big impact on your aim in FPS games.

What is DPI?

DPI describes how sensitive your mouse is. A higher DPI makes the cursor move farther with the same physical mouse movement.

What is Sensitivity?

Sensitivity is the setting inside the game that controls how quickly your view moves.

What is eDPI?

eDPI combines your mouse DPI and your in-game sensitivity.
